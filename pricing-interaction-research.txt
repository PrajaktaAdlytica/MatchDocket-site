MatchDocket pricing interactions — 13 October 2026

Research provider: configured Perplexity research tool, high preset.
Decision: preserve article containers and explicit destination links. Use a shallow 2px lift, soft elevation, a low-opacity warm surface wash and a burgundy top hairline. Keep hypothetical prices, qualifications and CTAs visible at rest. Provide focus-within emphasis and a separate link focus ring. Limit hover movement to fine pointers with hover support; remove lift and transitions in reduced-motion mode.

These visual treatments are original recommendations, not hover behavior verified on competitors. Research retrieval did not establish actual hover animations on Ramp, Numeric or Linear pricing pages.
