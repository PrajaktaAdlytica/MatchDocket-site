About MatchDocket

A better record for the decision in between.

Invoices do not become ready simply because their fields have been read. The questions between source documents, policy and approval deserve a clear record.

Read the researchRead the research
Our premise

Make exception adjudication a reviewable, evidence-linked process.

Human owners retain authority. They can inspect the source, question an assumption, reject a proposal and reverse a suggested change before it is executed.

01

Specific evidence.

Every proposed next step should point back to the record and policy that support it.

02

Accountable review.

A visible owner and dated next action matter more than an opaque automation claim.

03

Earned expansion.

Begin with one workflow. Broader use depends on reviewed usefulness, safe access and paid demand.

Where we are

A research-stage concept.

The site and product demo use fictional records. No customers, revenue, live integrations, certifications or measured savings are claimed.

The first mission is narrow: a permissioned read-only sample, one entity, one workflow and a proposed export. A paid pilot and agreed gates determine whether the concept proceeds.
