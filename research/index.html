Market context

Established alternatives set the starting line.

These vendor capabilities establish overlap. They do not prove demand for MatchDocket or superiority over an incumbent.

Invoice-mailbox monitoring, invoice preparation and supervisor review show that intake and draft creation already have native alternatives.

The open questions

Frequency, review time and integration cost.

How often does the exception recur in a specific ERP segment? How much evidence handling changes the review? Can a bounded pilot support a paid purchase decision?

Cross-entity policy reuse and cross-ERP expansion remain hypotheses. A polished demo alone does not answer them.

A smaller place to start

Help make the question specific.

Bring an exception type and a review owner. Keep raw confidential records outside the website.
