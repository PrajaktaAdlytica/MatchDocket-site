Frequently asked questions

A clear view of the scope.

What the concept does, what the synthetic demo shows and what a first pilot would need to establish.

Is MatchDocket an operating AP product?

No. This website presents a research-stage product concept and a synthetic demo. Production integrations, paid demand and pilot outcomes have not been demonstrated.

Why focus on exceptions instead of extraction?

Native ERP agents and established AP vendors already handle significant portions of capture, drafting, matching and approvals. The question to test is whether a focused evidence-and-review desk adds useful value on recurring exceptions.

What is in the first proposed pilot?

One selected workflow and one entity, using a permissioned read-only CSV sample of 30 de-identified invoices with matching POs and receipts. A reviewer sees evidence links, an exception queue and a proposed ERP-neutral draft export.

Can it change vendor bank details or make payments?

No. The preview cannot edit bank details, release a bank-change hard hold, transfer funds, send real messages or write to an ERP. A bank-change case remains held and requires independently owned verification.

Does “export-ready” mean approved for payment?

No. Export-ready means a review packet can be produced. AP-0418 can have an approved hold recommendation and an export-ready packet while its $600 discrepancy remains held.

How will a useful recommendation be measured?

Against actual AP decisions and the agreed policy, using blind human review. Track median reviewer minutes per exception and accepted resolutions, with false holds, missed duplicates and omissions measured separately.

Is the displayed pricing available now?

The $2,500 audit and $1,500–$4,000 per entity/month range are hypothetical prices to test willingness to pay. There is no checkout, active subscription or validated pricing benchmark.

Which ERPs does it integrate with?

Production connectors have not been demonstrated. The initial proposed scope uses a de-identified CSV and an ERP-neutral proposed export. Data access, mapping effort and sandbox fit need to be established for a pilot.

Can I upload an invoice here?

Please do not. This preview does not collect confidential documents. Describe the workflow in general terms; a permissioned data-handling plan is required before sharing a sample.

What would make the project proceed?

Confirmed repeat exceptions, a named approver who accepts the reviewed workflow, a safe sample or sandbox, agreed accuracy and security gates, and an authorized paid scoped pilot. If these are not available, refine the exception type or stop.
