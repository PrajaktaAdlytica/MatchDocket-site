Read-only starting scope

30 invoices. Matching evidence. One entity.

  1. 01

    Confirm authority and scope.

    Identify the data owner, selected exception workflow, entity, named reviewer and relevant policy. Establish permission, retention and handling before sharing a sample.

  2. 02

    Remove sensitive identifiers.

    Replace people, suppliers and entities with stable aliases. Remove bank details, tax identifiers, personal contact information and private document content that is not needed for the review.

  3. 03

    Preserve the relationships.

    Keep coherent pseudonymous invoice, PO, receipt and line IDs. Retain quantity, unit, unit price, totals, chronology and the links required to trace a comparison.

  4. 04

    Make assumptions explicit.

    Document tolerances, taxes, freight, discounts, returns, prior allocations and unit conversions where relevant. A reviewer should not need to guess the policy.

  5. 05

    Keep ground truth separate.

    Record the historical AP decision and reviewer time for later comparison. Blind review should not reveal the reference outcome to the person judging a new suggestion.

Thirty invoices may contain fewer than 20 actual exceptions. The validation target of 20+ manually adjudicated exceptions may require additional permissioned sample sets.
