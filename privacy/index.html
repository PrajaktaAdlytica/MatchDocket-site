Privacy

Clear handling for a limited preview.

This notice describes the local inquiry and synthetic demonstration behavior implemented on this website. An operational privacy policy needs review before real collection is enabled.

Concept-preview notice · 13 October 2026

Your inquiry stays local.

The inquiry form validates and creates a summary in the current page’s memory. It does not send the summary to MatchDocket. Saving a draft creates a file on your own device. Clear the draft or leave/reload the page to remove the in-page summary.

Use synthetic or general information.

Do not enter bank details, tax identifiers, invoices, employee information or confidential client records. The product demo uses fictional records; real records cannot be uploaded or connected.

Preview analytics.

The site records limited interaction events locally for implementation review, including page views, selected proposed modules, demo evidence views, price views and prepared inquiry consent. Contact fields and financial source data are not included in analytics event properties. No production analytics destination is configured in this preview.

Consent and updates.

The required checkbox explains the local inquiry purpose. A separate unchecked option can include interest in research updates in your draft. Neither option creates a subscription or transmits contact information.

Privacy and deletion route.

Use “Clear local draft” on the inquiry form to remove information held by that page. A downloaded file remains under your control and can be deleted from your device. For a privacy question, the contact preview can prepare a local message; it is not yet an operational submission endpoint.

Before operational collection.

A verified recipient, data controller details, retention policy, purpose-limited consent, processor arrangements and a working deletion route must be established before the site collects real inquiries or samples. Hosting providers may process technical access logs according to their own policies.

This notice is an implementation-specific preview statement, not a claim of certification or legal approval.
