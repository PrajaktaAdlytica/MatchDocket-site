Access before architecture

Fit is something to establish.

ERP access, source quality, mapping effort and implementation cost differ by environment. Production connectors and cross-ERP support have not been demonstrated.

  • Identify the current ERP and entity.
  • Confirm permission to share a de-identified sample.
  • Agree the field mapping and exception policy.
  • Use a sandbox before evaluating any future system write.
