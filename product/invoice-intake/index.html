A considered first look at every invoice.

The question to answer

Is this a duplicate, or a different billing period?

  • Compare invoice identity, amount and service period.
  • Link the candidate to its earlier source record.
  • Let an AP reviewer accept or reject the proposed hold.
The proposed output

An intake dossier with source references, identity questions and a proposed next action.
