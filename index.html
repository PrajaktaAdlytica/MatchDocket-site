A few things to know

Clear answers.
Clear boundaries.

What the concept proposes, what the preview can do, and what still needs evidence.

Read all questions
01Is MatchDocket a live AP product?+

No. This is a research-stage website and an isolated, fictional product preview. There are no live ERP connections, financial writes, payment execution, or customer outcomes to claim.

02How is this different from invoice capture?+

The proposed focus is evidence-linked exception resolution across workflows and, eventually, entities and ERPs. Native agents and established AP platforms already address capture, matching, and approval. Any incremental value must be proven in a scoped pilot.

03Can it change a vendor’s bank details?+

No. A bank-change request creates a hard hold and an independent callback task. The demo cannot alter a vendor master or treat a clicked button as verification.

04What would the first audit need?+

A permissioned, de-identified set of 30 invoices with matching PO and receipt records for one entity, plus the relevant policy and a named approver. Do not send sensitive records through this website.

05What counts as a successful pilot?+

Repeated exceptions, a workflow accepted by a named approver, safe data or sandbox access, agreed accuracy and security gates, and an authorized paid pilot. We would compare reviewer time and accepted resolutions against ground truth while tracking false holds and omissions.
