PROMPT 1
Use case: logo-brand. Design an original premium logo symbol for MatchDocket, B2B software for evidence-linked accounts-payable exception review. User liked warm ivory/olive editorial website and says the SYMBOLS were the problem with previous logos. Keep the elegant readable moderate-contrast serif MatchDocket wordmark from the reference style, but explore a genuinely fresh symbol. Exact brand spelling "MatchDocket", capital M/D. NO slogans.
Attached website image is ONLY palette and lettering context. Do not imitate its two-document placeholder icon. Absolutely NO initials/MD/M monograms, opposing square brackets, medical cross, two unequal upright bars, generic stock folder, generic bank building, money/coins, dollar sign, shield, tick, chart arrow, infinity, sparkle, leaf, or copied Numeric/Mercury logo.
One concept only on a 1536 x 1024 landscape presentation. Strong original graphic-design craft with controlled geometry, striking silhouette and generous negative space. Symbol must feel authored, not a familiar UI icon. Deep olive #26382D on warm ivory #F7F4ED. Crisp flat vector-like art. NO texture, gradient, 3D, embossing, shadows, stationery props, mockup scenes or faux pencil sketches.
Presentation: hero horizontal symbol + serif MatchDocket wordmark across upper half with ample margins; lower half three restrained applications of EXACT SAME symbol: icon-only large, reversed ivory on olive, and a small header lockup. Ensure all symbol applications geometrically identical. Tiny tasteful "Symbol study" and concept-name labels. No gratuitous anatomy grids, rulers, unapproved marketing statements or construction claims. Do not fake a tested16pxfavicon.
Concept name "Threefold". Create a compact asymmetric three-part emblem whose THREE SOLID FOLDED BANDS register neatly around one small open triangular junction. Three independent source records become a shared evidence case. Geometry based on a broad hexagonal footprint but NO enclosing hexagon, shield, flower or circular petals. It should resemble an elegantly folded continuous sequence of paper margins rather than three arrows spinning. Wide coherent bands, optically balanced negative channels, one deliberate offset giving a memorable silhouette. No long pointed arrowheads. Folds expressed in silhouette and negative space only, no shading. The emblem must be simple enough to draw from memory, somewhat like an original editorial publisher's mark. Not a generic pinwheel, recycle triangle or blockchain hexagon. The three modules have clear straight bases and gently clipped outer corners; central whitespace forms a small three-way registration point. Prioritize beautiful distinctive drawn form over a literal product pictogram.
