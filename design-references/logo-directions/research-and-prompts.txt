Research findings and primary sources

1. Distinctiveness should arise from the product's own idea. Pentagram's Droit identity relates custom typography to transformation and decision systems; OpenView develops custom lettering into a monogram and a broader icon language. These case studies inform a method, not letterforms to copy.
https://www.pentagram.com/work/droit
https://www.pentagram.com/work/openview/story

2. Simplify forms for digital use. Pentagram's Mastercard work discusses geometry and digital applications. The lesson for MatchDocket is to create a stable recognizable silhouette, not to reuse interlocking circles or the payment network's identity.
https://www.pentagram.com/work/mastercard

3. Define placement, clear space, contrast and appropriate positive/reversed variants. Mastercard's brand requirements provide a precise example of application rules. Its numeric dimensions, colors and restrictions are specific to Mastercard, not generic finance-logo standards.
https://www.mastercard.com/brandcenter/us/en/brand-requirements/mastercard.html

4. Treat icon and full logo as different assets. Stripe's account-branding documentation distinguishes square digital icons from non-square logos and their different invoice placements. This supports planning both a compact MatchDocket mark and horizontal wordmark; it does not establish a Stripe integration.
https://docs.stripe.com/get-started/account/branding

5. A browser favicon appears at very small size. MDN describes the common 16px favicon. Actual 16px and 32px tests are required after the selected mark is drawn in vector; the concept sheets only illustrate icon and lockup applications.
https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Glossary/Favicon

The Perplexity research returned a Wise guideline URL, but direct verification redirected to a 404. No Wise-specific sizing or optical rules have been relied upon here.

Design synthesis for MatchDocket

- Pair a compact mark with readable editorial lettering.
- Preserve the selected ivory and deep olive aesthetic.
- Use matching, folios, docket indexing or initials as the concept vocabulary.
- Avoid default consumer finance symbols and generic growth claims.
- Keep brand marks simpler than the detailed document illustrations on the website.
- Optical spacing, real-size legibility, consistent single-color geometry and reversed artwork need production refinement.
- Color and a polished mark do not themselves establish security, reliability or trust.

Concepts and recommendation

01 Aligned Folios: two solid upright forms and a shared matching seam. Most directly connected to the dossier concept, although the silhouette can also read as an architectural form.
02 Evidence Join: two opposing docket-edge forms around open negative space. More abstract; the revised version removes an unrequested slogan. The small/reverse depictions still need geometric normalization before production.
03 Docket Monogram: editorial MD monogram paired with the full name. Recommended as the strongest fit for the selected website typography. The combined initials need optical adjustment at favicon scale.
04 Matched Wordmark: restrained wordmark with related M icon. Quietest expression; the proposed distinctive letter details need stronger vector development to avoid a stock-font impression.

Presentation labels and any provisional slogans are not part of an approved final logo. The full name remains MatchDocket.

Next production pass after selection

Draw the chosen geometry in editable vector, normalize all lockups, refine lettering and kerning, check originality/similarity, and test the mark at 16/32px plus the real website header size. Produce horizontal, icon-only, monochrome and reversed versions and an actual favicon. No trademark clearance is represented by this exploration.
